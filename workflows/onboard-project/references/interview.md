# Onboard Interview (v3)

Use ask() (rec first) for:

Each vision, persona, success, technology, deployment, priority, or already-initialized delegate-or-exit `question` includes a short paragraph stating the situation and the facts needed to choose among the shown options. Do not paste a full product vision or repository dump into `question`.

- product vision / personas / success criteria (1 ask)

- tech: language, framework, test, deploy (1 ask)

- optional 3rd for priorities or notes.
